# Changelog

## 0.3.1 / Android 0.1.1

- Made 3D studies the first and default lesson tab on web and Android, with source demonstrations one tab away.
- Kept sections without a 3D study explicit, with a direct link to their demonstration.

## 0.3.0

- Made the source demonstration the default lesson view. All 21 lessons and 150 sections retain their order, instructions, and source timestamps.
- Withdrew the unreliable generated human motion, including the archer weave. Full-body 3D reconstruction remains unfinished. The 3D viewer now presents prop geometry and static spatial references separately from technique instruction.
- Added source-segment scrubbing, 0.1-second steps, theater and full screen, plus a live close-up for downloaded videos. The close-up preserves the original frame orientation and supports area selection, pan, and zoom.
- Rebuilt the geometric studies with rigid staves in separate planes. Corrected reverse playback paths and added complete-catalog shaft-separation checks.
- Added source-timestamped movement sequences for every lesson section, including thumb/pinky inside-versus-outside elbow passages.
- Standardized both staff end shapes, black shafts and grips, and white center bands. Added independent left/right staff visibility and clearer perspective references.
- Removed playback bars from static references. Multi-position studies retain previous/next controls.
- Added the hosted website link prominently to both READMEs.

## Android 0.1.0

- Added native Android navigation, search, progress, saved sections, and outdoor display settings.
- Bundled the complete offline curriculum, shared 3D studies, motion lab, VTG reference, and source notes.
- Added native local source-video playback with segment loops, seeking, stepping, speed, full screen, and a video close-up.
- Added signed APK distribution through GitHub Releases and Klobstore. Google Play publication remains planned separately.

## 0.2.0

- Replaced fixed diagram views with a depth-tested 3D viewer, an isometric default, free orbit/pan/zoom, theater mode, and full screen.
- Added colored thumb and pinky tips, visible finger placement, and a live grip inset for either hand.
- Separated coincident hand planes so together-same patterns show two staves. Added overlap labels and a see-through body option.
- Animated the archer shoulder and underarm repeat from source checkpoints. Joint positions and clearance distances remain schematic; the brief side transfer uses endpoint setups.
- Removed promotional copy, collapsed secondary controls and long section lists, and changed dark mode to charcoal with blue accents.
- Recorded the planned Android app identity and content-parity requirements.

## 0.1.1

Added dark mode as the default, with a persistent header toggle. The full interface, animated staffs, body guides, grip diagrams, and VTG illustrations share the theme palette. The saved choice applies before the page paints and syncs between open tabs.

## 0.1.0

The first complete curriculum release covers both requested DrexFactor playlists, with 21 lesson pages and 150 ordered move and study sections.

- Added a responsive lesson library, source timestamps, grip and stance cues, prerequisites, practice records, and move search.
- Added geometric diagrams and discrete pose/spatial studies with seeking, stepping, looping, automatic move advancement, adjustable speed, and multiple views.
- Added YouTube source playback and private, browser-local video selection. Local development can play the downloaded research videos directly.
- Added the motion lab and VTG reference, with attribution to the videos and supplied poster.
- Added source coverage, motion constraint, and browser checks.

Complex body movements use verified checkpoints or source playback where a continuous diagram has not been established. Downloaded tutorials and authentication material are not included in the release.
